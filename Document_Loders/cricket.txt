CRICKET KNOWLEDGE BASE
======================

1. INTRODUCTION

Cricket is a bat-and-ball sport played between two teams, each consisting
of 11 players. The game is played on a circular or oval-shaped ground
with a rectangular pitch in the centre.

The main objective is to score more runs than the opposing team.

Each team takes turns batting and bowling. The batting team tries to
score runs, while the bowling team attempts to take wickets and restrict
the opposition's score.

2. BASIC RULES

- Each team has 11 players.
- An over consists of 6 legal deliveries.
- A cricket pitch is 22 yards long.
- Two batters are on the field during normal play.
- A batter can score runs by running between the wickets or hitting
  boundaries.
- A team loses a wicket when a batter gets dismissed.
- The team with the higher score generally wins.

3. TYPES OF CRICKET FORMATS

TEST CRICKET:
- Can last up to 5 days.
- Each team normally gets two innings.
- Focuses on patience, technique and strategy.

ONE DAY INTERNATIONAL (ODI):
- Each team gets a maximum of 50 overs.
- Usually completed in one day.
- Combines attacking and defensive batting.

T20 CRICKET:
- Each team gets a maximum of 20 overs.
- A fast-paced format.
- Encourages aggressive batting and innovative bowling.

4. METHODS OF SCORING

- Single: One run by running between the wickets.
- Double: Two runs by running between the wickets.
- Triple: Three runs by running between the wickets.
- Boundary (Four): Ball reaches the boundary after touching the ground.
- Six: Ball crosses the boundary without touching the ground.
- Extras: Additional runs from wides, no-balls, byes and leg-byes.

5. TYPES OF DISMISSALS

BOWLED:
The batter is dismissed when the ball hits the stumps and dislodges
the bails.

CAUGHT:
The batter is dismissed when a fielder catches the ball before it
touches the ground.

LBW:
Leg Before Wicket. The batter is dismissed when the ball hits their
body instead of the bat and would have hit the stumps, subject to
the LBW rules.

RUN OUT:
A batter is dismissed when the fielding team breaks the wicket while
the batter is outside the crease and not safely making their ground.

STUMPED:
The wicketkeeper breaks the wicket while the batter is outside the
crease and not attempting a run, subject to the rules.

6. IMPORTANT CRICKET TERMS

- Wicket: The three stumps and two bails.
- Over: Six legal deliveries bowled by one bowler.
- Maiden Over: An over in which no runs are charged to the bowler.
- Run Rate: Average runs scored per over.
- Strike Rate: Runs scored per 100 balls faced by a batter.
- Economy Rate: Average runs conceded per over by a bowler.
- Partnership: Runs scored by two batters together.
- Powerplay: Overs with fielding restrictions.
- All-rounder: A player skilled in both batting and bowling.
- Hat-trick: Taking three wickets in three consecutive deliveries.
